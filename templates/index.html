JUSTO Legal Procedure Assistant

Grounded in Uganda's Local Council Courts Act, 2006 to prevent unlawful fees, invalid quorum rulings, and missed appeal deadlines.

Key Statutory Safeguards Built Into JUSTO

  • Section 8 (Quorum): Minimum 5 members sitting, including at least 2 women.
  • Section 10 (Jurisdiction): Civil debts/contracts up to UGX 2,000,000 (100 currency points) and customary disputes; blocks criminal felonies and registered land titles.
  • Section 32 (Appeals): Standardized, hash-verified judgment record issued within the 14-day statutory appeal window.
